How it works

From your first word to fluency

  1. Choose a languageGerman and French each have their own page and their own progress.
  2. Find your levelNever studied it? Begin with the Starter: first words with audio. Know some already? Take the placement check.
  3. Follow the lesson planSix lessons per level, in two sub-levels: goals, grammar, vocabulary, speaking and writing tasks.
  4. Practice and testEach lesson opens ten maze rooms and ends with a unit test.
